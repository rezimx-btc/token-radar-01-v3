How the model works

implied = TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity × chains

  • Category multiple. Median market-cap / TVL of the category’s live tokens, shrunk toward the global median with a prior of 5, clamped to 0.05–2.5. A category with fewer than 2 live tokens never sets its own price — it uses the global median, so no single outlier can price a category.
  • Growth. 1 + clamp(7d change, −50…+150%) / 100 × 0.6.
  • Maturity. 0.6 + 0.4 × min(age, 1460 days) / 1460 — age matters more than in the first radar.
  • Chains. 1 + 0.04 per extra chain, capped at five extras.
  • Floor & cap. Implied value is at least $1 and at most $100,000,000,000,000.
